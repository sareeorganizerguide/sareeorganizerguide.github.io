## Storage Tips

### Use Breathable Covers

Store sarees in cotton or muslin covers whenever possible.

### Avoid Plastic Storage

Long-term plastic storage can trap moisture and damage fabrics.

### Refold Regularly

Change folds periodically to prevent permanent creases.

### Protect Against Moisture

Store sarees in dry and well-ventilated areas.

### Keep Away From Direct Sunlight

Sunlight can fade colors and weaken delicate fabrics.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best way to store silk sarees?

Silk sarees should be stored in breathable fabric covers and refolded periodically.

### How often should sarees be refolded?

Every 3–6 months is generally recommended.

### Can sarees be stored in plastic covers?

For short-term storage, yes. For long-term storage, breathable fabric covers are preferred.

### How can I prevent moisture damage?

Store sarees in dry areas and use moisture absorbers when necessary.
