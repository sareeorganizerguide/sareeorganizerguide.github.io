Privacy Policy

How we handle your data — plain and simple.

Last updated: May 26, 2026

Who We Are

OrganizeYourSaree (www.yoursite.com) is an independent review site that tests saree storage organisers. We're based in India and write for Indian audiences.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information directly. We do not have a newsletter, user accounts, or registration forms.

If you contact us via email, we'll have whatever information you share in that message. We don't store it in any database — it stays in our email inbox.

Cookies & Analytics

We use cookies for:

  • Google Analytics: Tracks page views, time on site, and traffic sources. This data is anonymous — we can't identify individual visitors. We use it to understand which content is helpful and where our traffic comes from.
  • Amazon Affiliate Cookies: When you click an Amazon link on our site, Amazon may set a cookie to track that you came from us. This is how we earn commissions — at no cost to you.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Our site will still work.

Affiliate Links

Some links on this site are affiliate links (primarily Amazon India). If you click these links and make a purchase, we earn a small commission. This does not affect the price you pay.

Our product recommendations are based on testing, not affiliate commissions. We recommend products we've genuinely tested and believe are worth buying.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics: Web analytics. Google's Privacy Policy
  • Amazon Associates: Affiliate program. Amazon's Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts: Typography. Fonts are loaded from Google's CDN.

Data Retention

Analytics data is retained by Google Analytics according to their default settings (26 months). We don't store any personal data on our own servers.

Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal data directly, there's nothing for us to delete or modify. If you have concerns about data collected by our third-party services (Google, Amazon), please refer to their respective privacy policies linked above.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top. We won't notify users individually — this page is always the current version.
